### A. Continuous Dual Metrology
Every physical measurement in the English text displays its dual equivalence:
1. **Temperatures:**
   - Simmering water: $85^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($185^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
   - Full rolling boil: $100^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($212^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
   - Maillard reaction threshold: $140\text{--}165^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($285\text{--}330^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
   - Standard cake baking: $180^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($350^\circ\mathrm{F}$ / Gas Mark 4)
   - High roasting: $200\text{--}220^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($392\text{--}428^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
   - Cast-iron searing: $230\text{--}250^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($450\text{--}482^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
   - Clarified butter / Ghee smoke point: $250^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($482^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
   - Refined avocado oil smoke point: $270^\circ\mathrm{C}$ ($520^\circ\mathrm{F}$)
2. **Masses & Volumes:**
   - $100\,\mathrm{g}$ dry pasta $\approx 3.5\,\mathrm{oz}$
   - $300\,\mathrm{g}$ steak $\approx 10.5\,\mathrm{oz}$
   - $1\,\mathrm{liter}$ liquid $\approx 1.05\,\mathrm{quarts}$ (or $4.2\,\mathrm{cups}$)
   - $250\,\mathrm{ml}$ broth $\approx 1\,\mathrm{cup}$ ($8.5\,\mathrm{fl\ oz}$)
   - $10\,\mathrm{g}$ fine sea salt $\approx 1.7\,\mathrm{teaspoons}$ (or $1\,\mathrm{tablespoon}$ coarse kosher salt)
   - $90\,\mathrm{g}$ butter $\approx 6.3\,\mathrm{tablespoons}$ (or $0.8\,\mathrm{stick}$)
   - $200\,\mathrm{g}$ chocolate $\approx 7\,\mathrm{oz}$

### B. Core Ingredient Adaptations
1. **Butter Chemistry (82% vs. 80% Butterfat):**
   - French/European butter legally contains **minimum 82% butterfat** ($\le 16\%$ water).
   - Standard US butter (USDA grade) contains **80% butterfat** ($\approx 18\%$ water).
   - *Physical impact:* American commercial butter adds more free water to pastry and sauces, accelerating starch gelatinization or wetting flaky crusts. Recommend European-style butter (Kerrygold, Plugrá, cultured butter) or reduce liquid hydration by 1 to 2 tablespoons per 100 g butter.
2. **Cereal Flour Classifications:**
   - France classifies flour by **ash content** (mineral residue after incineration at $900^\circ\mathrm{C}$): T45, T55, T65, T80, T110, T150.
   - English-speaking countries classify flour by **protein/gluten content**:
     - T45 ($\approx 9\%$ protein) $\to$ *Pastry Flour* / *Cake Flour*.
     - T55 ($10\text{--}11\%$ protein) $\to$ *All-Purpose Flour* (AP Flour).
     - T65 ($12\text{--}13\%$ protein) $\to$ *Bread Flour*.
     - T150 $\to$ *Whole Wheat Flour*.
3. **Egg Sizing:**
   - Europe: Medium ($53\text{--}63\,\mathrm{g}$), Large ($63\text{--}73\,\mathrm{g}$).
   - United States: *Large Egg* ($50\,\mathrm{g}$ without shell, $\approx 57\,\mathrm{g}$ whole).
4. **Butchery: French Anatomical Muscle Cuts vs. Anglo-American Primal Cuts:**
   - French butchery isolates individual muscles along connective seams (e.g., *paleron*, *gîte*, *bavette*, *araignée*, *onglet*).
   - Anglo-American butchery prioritizes cross-sectional bone-in blocks (*Chuck*, *Rib*, *Loin*, *Round*, *Brisket*, *Plate*).
